Frequently Asked Questions

How do I build my fortune?

Start with $47. Work, invest, build businesses, and make financial decisions to grow your empire.

Can I continue my family legacy?

Yes. Build a family, raise children, and voluntarily pass your empire to an eligible adult biological child.

How do I save my game?

WEALTHBORN saves progress locally on your device. Avoid deleting the app if you want to preserve your progress.

Does WEALTHBORN require an internet connection?

The core game can be played offline.
