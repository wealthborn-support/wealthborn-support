# WEALTHBORN Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 22, 2026**

WEALTHBORN is a single-player mobile simulation game.

## Information We Collect

WEALTHBORN does not collect personal information, account information, location data, or device identifiers.

## Game Progress

Game progress and saved data are stored locally on your device. WEALTHBORN does not transmit your saved game data to our servers.

## Analytics and Advertising

WEALTHBORN does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

## Third-Party Services

WEALTHBORN does not share user data with third-party services.

## Children's Privacy

WEALTHBORN does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, please visit our [WEALTHBORN Support page](https://wealthborn-support.github.io/wealthborn-support/).
