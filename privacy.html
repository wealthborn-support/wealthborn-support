WEALTHBORN Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

WEALTHBORN is a single-player mobile wealth and life simulation game.

Information We Collect

WEALTHBORN does not collect personal information, account information, location data, or device identifiers.

Game Progress

Game progress is stored locally on your device. WEALTHBORN does not transmit saved game data to our servers.

Analytics and Advertising

WEALTHBORN does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Third-Party Services

WEALTHBORN does not share user data with third-party services.

Children's Privacy

WEALTHBORN does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the game's data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit our WEALTHBORN Support page.
